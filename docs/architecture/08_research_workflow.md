# Research Workflow Documentation

1. **Generate** research candidates (P29) from historical knowledge (READ ONLY).
2. **Interpret** and connect insights (P28) over memory (P27).
3. **Meta-analyze** the research process (P30) — observations only.
4. **Orchestrate** experiments (P31) — records only, human approval required, never executes.
5. **Track** resources (P32) — records only, never allocates/provisions.
6. **Expose** read-only services (P33) and **aggregate** dashboards (P34).
7. **Validate** the whole ecosystem (P35).

At no point does the platform execute trades, deploy strategies, or make autonomous
investment decisions. All outputs are research artifacts.
